## Notes

AEDT (UTC+11:00) is observed in New South Wales, Victoria, Tasmania and the
Australian Capital Territory during daylight saving time. Outside of daylight
saving time these regions use AEST (UTC+10:00).

This file records a point in time and is not updated automatically.
